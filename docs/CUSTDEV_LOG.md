workflow. Record evidence against these questions, with private contact details kept separately:

| Question | Interview IDs / observation | Decision it informs |
|---|---|---|
| Who records arrival and who can accept quantity/condition? What happens on the next shift? | Not collected | Named actors and responsibility |
| Show the last short, late or disputed delivery and how it was resolved. | Not collected | Whether an evidence packet is useful |
| Which data already exists in 1C, documents or bank reports, and when is it available? | Not collected | Whether capture adds useful information |
| Can the receiver use a phone, take a photo and work when connectivity fails? How long can recording take? | Not collected | Capture burden and offline scope |
| What units, partial loads and reference mismatches occur? | Not collected | First material family and data contract |
| How will all arrivals be counted independently of app use? | Not collected | Honest coverage denominator |
| Who would approve a 30-day pilot and a priced continuation? | Not collected | Host and buyer gate |
| For a financial institution: would a named team evaluate a particular receipt packet, and for which decision? | Not collected | Later evidence-consumer gate |
