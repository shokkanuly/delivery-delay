# 07 · Visibility plan: how to become known to the companies this serves

Goal: make the right people in Kazakhstani construction, logistics and finance find this work, trust it and talk to you. Stars are not customers; the plan measures conversations and pilots first.

## 1. Overview

- **Campaign name:** Delivery Truth KZ.
- **One sentence:** Publish open, sourced research and real pilot numbers on what physically arrives on construction sites, so builders, suppliers and banks come to you.
- **Primary objective (90 days):** 10 interviews, 3 pilot letters of intent, 1 live pilot with the gap-test dataset, 1 bank or MFO conversation, 1 published case study.
- **Secondary:** 5 inbound messages from target companies; repo traffic from Kazakhstan; one media mention.

## 2. Audience

| Segment | Who | Pain | Where they are |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Project managers, site managers, procurement heads at mid-size contractors in Astana and Almaty | Late or disputed deliveries, idle cranes, manual reconciliation | LinkedIn, Telegram groups, AstanaBuild, site visits |
| Secondary | Commercial directors at material suppliers; carriers with 3.5 t+ fleets | Slow payment, disputes, ЕСУТД deadline | Telegram, trade shows, supplier directories |
| Tertiary | Bank factoring and SME teams, MFOs, BI Group and MOST innovation teams, ministry digitalisation staff | Risk on financed invoices, digital construction targets | Scouting programs, AIFC Tech Hub, Astana Hub, conferences |

## 3. Messages

- **Core:** Know what actually arrived, and when, before the invoice is disputed.
- **For builders:** Fewer idle cranes and a clear record of who was late.
- **For suppliers:** Proof of delivery that gets you paid sooner.
- **For carriers:** Ready for ЕСУТД and proof of waiting time.
- **For banks:** An independent delivery record you can check, with consent.
- **Proof points:** gap-test numbers, pilot results, the public sources in this repo, the open method for supplier scores.
- **Never say:** "banks do not have X" until you have checked. Say "we are testing whether X is missing".

## 7. GitHub tactics

- Set topics: `kazakhstan`, `logistics`, `fintech`, `energy`, `construction-tech`, `supply-chain`, `open-data`, `research`.
- Write a one-line repo description and pin the repo on your profile.
- Add a social preview image (export the map).
- Tag releases (`v0.1`) and keep a `CHANGELOG.md` that shows corrections.
- Add issue templates: "I disagree", "I have data", "I want a pilot".
- Turn on Discussions; post a "problem of the week".
- Add a `CITATION.cff` so people can cite it.
- Link the repo from your LinkedIn, your pitch decks and your applications.
- Ask partners, mentors and judges to link back if they agree with the work.
- Keep the CSV stable so others can reuse it.

## 8. Outreach templates (adapt, send one at a time, personalise)

**To the judge:**
> Здравствуйте, [имя]. Спасибо за обратную связь на финале. Вы сказали, что банки уже получают отчёты и документы по сделкам компаний. Можно 15 минут, чтобы понять, каких данных там не хватает, особенно по фактическому приезду поставок на объект? Я собрал открытый разбор проблемы: [ссылка].

**To a site or project manager:**
> Здравствуйте, [имя]. Мы тестируем простой способ фиксировать приезд поставок на объект: QR, фото и время, работает без интернета на воротах. Ищем один объект в Астане на 30 дней бесплатного пилота. Менять 1С не нужно. Можно 15 минут на этой неделе?

**To a supplier:**
> Здравствуйте, [имя]. Как быстро вы получаете оплату после поставки на объект и как часто возникают споры о количестве или времени? Мы проверяем, поможет ли независимая запись о приезде машины. Могу рассказать за 15 минут.

**To a bank or MFO:**
> Здравствуйте, [имя]. Мы изучаем, как подтверждать фактическую поставку материалов при факторинге в строительстве. Какие данные вы проверяете до финансирования, и принял бы ваш андеррайтинг независимую запись о поставке с согласия клиента? Буду благодарен за 20 минут.

## 9. Interview questions

- **Site managers:** How do you confirm a delivery today? How often is it late or short? What does a day of delay cost? Who settles disputes, and how long does it take?
- **Suppliers:** How long until you are paid? How often is a delivery disputed? Would you pay for proof the buyer and the bank accept?
- **Carriers:** How long do you wait at the gate? What worries you about ЕСУТД? What would make a driver app worth using?
- **Banks:** What delivery data do you check before financing? What went wrong in the last bad case? Who decides on a new data source?

## 11. Rules for yourself

1. Get written permission before naming a company or publishing its data.
2. Anonymise drivers and workers; collect the minimum personal data and have consent.
3. Do not send bulk or scraped messages; one honest message at a time.
4. Say what you have tested and what you have not.
5. Do not claim a partnership you do not have.
6. Check advertising and personal-data rules for the channel you use. This is not legal advice.

## 12. Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| No pilot site agrees | Offer smaller scope (one gate, one week); use interviews to adjust the pitch; ask the judge and mentors for introductions |
| Gap test shows no gap | Pivot the story to operations (queues, cranes) for developers; publish the honest result anyway |
| Big players build it first | Be the neutral evidence layer; integrate rather than compete; partner with a bank |
| Poor data from drivers' phones | Offline-first design; simple flow; optional BLE tag |
| Lost time on marketing | Cap outreach to a fixed weekly block; build only after a pilot asks |
