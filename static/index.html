Enterprise Deployment · Dual-Site Engagement Framework

Pilot Engagement, Data Ingestion & Commercial Terms

A structured 6–8 week dual-site trial designed to calibrate the gradient-boosting delay-risk model against client historical ERP logs, validate CP-SAT conflict-free crane scheduling in active site operations, and prove cost avoidance prior to enterprise-wide rollout.

4-Phase Deployment Roadmap (6–8 Weeks)
Phase 1 · Weeks 1–2
Data Ingestion & Baseline Calibration
• Ingest 12+ months historical 1C/ERP delivery logs
• Calibrate supplier delay priors and transit routes
• Map laydown staging gates and hoist/crane constraints
• Establish baseline delay frequency and conflict rate
Phase 2 · Weeks 3–4
Shadow Dispatch Mode
• Run daily delivery schedules in parallel with existing team
• Compare CP-SAT slot recommendations against manual dispatch
• Track false-positive / false-negative alerts on real arrivals
• Fine-tune weather gating thresholds with site engineers
Phase 3 · Weeks 5–6
Active Gatekeeper Pilot
• Pilot site logistics superintendents use SitePulse for daily dispatch
• Dispatch shared equipment with no double-booked units (CP-SAT)
• Automated 48-hour risk flags sent to supplier reps
• Weekly logistics conflict audit & resolution review
Phase 4 · Weeks 7–8
Executive Audit & Production Rollout
• Audit measured idle time reductions and avoided demurrage
• Compare protected workfront volume vs shadow control site
• Present validated ROI scorecard to holding leadership
• Execute enterprise multi-site rollout agreement
What the Client Provides
1. ERP / 1C Delivery Records (12 mo): Supplier name, ordered vs actual arrival timestamp, material code, quantity, and demurrage incidents.
2. Tower Crane & Machinery Manifest: Crane coordinates, lift radius envelopes, hook capacity, hook operation hours, and daily rental rates.
3. Project Master Schedule (Primavera / MS Project / Excel): Phase milestones, planned pour sequences, and structural handover dates.
4. Site Access Rules: Gate locations, staging pad capacities, and municipal heavy-transport curfew windows.
What SitePulse Delivers
1. Calibrated Risk Model: Gradient-boosting model + Bayesian shrinkage parameters retrained on the regional supplier base.
2. Automated Daily Dispatch Board: Equipment plan with no double-booked units (CP-SAT solver).
3. Phase-Gate Sequence Guard: flags every delivery dated before its build phase, before dispatch.
4. Executive ROI & Idle Reduction Audit: Bi-weekly verified reports quantifying hours saved, demurrage avoided, and concrete batches protected.
